# Research and design sources
Verified September 27, 2026. Sources support context and product hypotheses, not product efficacy claims.

## Sector need
LSC, 2022 Justice Gap Study, using 2021 survey/intake data:
https://justicegap.lsc.gov/resource/section-1-introduction/
- 92%: substantial civil legal problems with no or insufficient help among low-income Americans.
- 71%: civil legal problems brought to LSC-funded organizations not fully served.
These describe different denominators, not a before/after comparison.

## Workflow evidence
LSC, Online Intake & Triage, historical TIG project 07193:
https://www.lsc.gov/i-am-grantee/grantee-guidance/lsc-reporting-requirements/tig-reporting/online-intake-triage
Legal Aid of Western Ohio reported roughly 10–15 staff minutes saved per application acceptance. This is historical program-specific evidence, not a universal benchmark or a result of Aid Atlas. Calculator uses 12 minutes as an editable illustration; other defaults are assumptions.
